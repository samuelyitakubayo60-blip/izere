Sexual Health

STIs & STDs

Sexually transmitted infections are common and treatable. Knowledge, testing, and prevention protect you and your partners.

Prevention Saves Lives Testing is Confidential No Shame, No Judgment
⚠️ Important Reminder

Many STIs have no visible symptoms but can still be passed to others and cause serious health problems if untreated. Regular testing is the only way to know your status. There is no shame in getting tested — it is a sign of responsibility.

🦠 What Are STIs?

Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs), also called Sexually Transmitted Diseases (STDs), are infections passed from person to person primarily through sexual contact — vaginal, anal, or oral sex.

How STIs Are Transmitted
  • Unprotected vaginal, anal, or oral sex
  • Sharing needles or sharp objects
  • Blood transfusion with infected blood
  • From mother to child during pregnancy, birth, or breastfeeding
  • Some STIs (like herpes) can spread through skin-to-skin contact

📋 Common STIs

🔴 VIRAL

HIV / AIDS

Attacks the immune system. No cure, but highly manageable with antiretroviral therapy (ART). People on treatment live long, healthy lives. Prevented with condoms, PrEP, and regular testing.

🟡 VIRAL

HPV (Human Papillomavirus)

Most common STI. Can cause genital warts and some cancers (cervical, anal). Often has no symptoms. HPV vaccine is available and highly effective — ask your health center.

🟣 VIRAL

Herpes (HSV-1 & HSV-2)

Causes painful blisters or sores on mouth or genitals. No cure, but antivirals reduce outbreaks and transmission risk. Very common — can be managed well.

🔵 BACTERIAL

Gonorrhea

Causes thick discharge, painful urination. Can be cured with antibiotics. If untreated, can cause infertility. Drug-resistant gonorrhea is an increasing concern.

🟢 BACTERIAL

Chlamydia

Often has NO symptoms — most common curable STI. Can cause infertility if left untreated. Easily cured with a single course of antibiotics. Testing is simple and painless.

🩷 BACTERIAL

Syphilis

Starts with painless sores, progresses in stages. Curable with penicillin if caught early. Can cause serious organ damage and be passed to unborn babies if untreated.

🔍 Common Signs & Symptoms

These symptoms may indicate an STI. If you notice any of them, get tested promptly — do not wait.

In Both Men & Women
  • Unusual discharge from genitals
  • Pain or burning during urination
  • Sores, blisters, or bumps on genitals, mouth, or anus
  • Rash on hands, feet, or body
  • Abnormal growths (warts)
  • Itching or irritation in genital area
Additional Signs in Women
  • Unusual vaginal discharge (color, smell)
  • Bleeding between periods
  • Pain during sex
  • Lower abdominal pain
  • Burning or swelling
🚨 Remember

Many people with STIs — especially chlamydia and HIV in early stages — have NO symptoms at all. The only way to know for sure is to get tested.

🛡️ How to Prevent STIs

🔵
Use Condoms Correctly

The most effective way to prevent STIs during sex. Use for vaginal, anal, and oral sex.

🧪
Get Tested Regularly

If sexually active, get tested at least once a year. Free at Rwanda health centers.

💉
Get Vaccinated

HPV vaccine protects against cervical cancer. Available at health centers.

🛡️
PrEP for HIV

If at high risk for HIV, take PrEP daily. Very effective when taken consistently.

🤝
Communicate with Partners

Talk openly with partners about testing and protection. Mutual testing builds trust.

🚫
Avoid Sharing Sharp Items

Never share needles, razors, or scissors. HIV and hepatitis can spread this way.

🧬 Testing & Treatment

✅ What Can Be Cured
  • Gonorrhea — antibiotics
  • Chlamydia — antibiotics
  • Syphilis — penicillin
  • Trichomoniasis — antibiotics
  • Most bacterial STIs — curable if treated early
💊 What Can Be Managed
  • HIV — ART therapy → undetectable = untransmittable
  • Herpes — antivirals reduce outbreaks
  • HPV — warts treated, cancers screened
  • Early treatment prevents serious complications
📍 Where to Get Tested in Rwanda
  • All public health centers offer free HIV testing
  • District hospitals offer full STI screening
  • Youth-friendly health units in many districts
  • Testing is confidential — your results are private
  • Call 114 for your nearest testing center

❌ Myths vs Facts

❌ MYTH

"You can tell if someone has an STI just by looking at them."

✅ FACT

Most STIs have no visible symptoms. You cannot tell — only testing confirms it.

❌ MYTH

"Only promiscuous people get STIs."

✅ FACT

Anyone who is sexually active can get an STI — even from a single encounter or a long-term partner.

❌ MYTH

"HIV is a death sentence."

✅ FACT

People with HIV on ART treatment live long, healthy lives. Early diagnosis and treatment make all the difference.

❌ MYTH

"Getting tested means you don't trust your partner."

✅ FACT

Regular testing is a responsible health habit — like annual check-ups. It shows you care about your health and theirs.
