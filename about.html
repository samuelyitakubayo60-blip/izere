Our Story

About IBANGA

"Ibanga" means privacy / confidentiality in Kinyarwanda — perfectly capturing our commitment to safe, anonymous, and trustworthy sexual and reproductive health support for Rwandan youth.

🎯

Our Mission

To improve access to accurate, confidential, and youth-friendly sexual and reproductive health information among young people in Rwanda through an AI-powered, Kinyarwanda-speaking chatbot available on WhatsApp, SMS, and the web — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

🌟

Our Vision

A Rwanda where every young person — regardless of location, disability, literacy level, or socioeconomic background — has equal access to reliable SRH information, can make informed health decisions, and feels empowered to seek care without fear or stigma.

❓ The Problem We're Solving

In Rwanda, young people aged 15–30 face multiple barriers when accessing sexual and reproductive health information and services:

😔
Stigma & Shame

Fear of judgment stops youth from seeking help at clinics or from family members.

🔒
Lack of Privacy

Health facilities often lack confidentiality, making sensitive questions uncomfortable.

📵
Misinformation

Myths from peers and social media create false beliefs about HIV, contraception, and STIs.

🌍
Geographic Barriers

Rural youth face long distances to clinics, reducing access to SRH services.

🗣️
Language Gap

Most digital health resources are in English or French — not Kinyarwanda.

♿
Exclusion of PWD

Youth with disabilities are often entirely left out of SRH education and services.

Inclusive Design

Disability Inclusion & Accessibility

Young people with disabilities face even greater barriers to SRH services. IBANGA is specifically designed to include them — not as an afterthought, but as a core design priority.

👂
Hearing Impairments

Fully text-based on WhatsApp, SMS, and web — no voice calls required. People who are Deaf or hard of hearing can use all features independently.

👁️
Visual Impairments

High-contrast mode, large text option, and screen-reader compatible HTML structure. All images have descriptive alt text.

🦽
Mobility / Physical Challenges

Access reliable health information from home — eliminating the need to physically travel to clinics for basic questions. Full keyboard navigation support.

💬
Speech Impairments

Text-based interaction and predefined quick-reply buttons minimize the need for typing, giving users with speech difficulties full independence.

🧠
Learning Differences

Simple Language Mode in the chatbot uses plain, short Kinyarwanda sentences instead of complex medical terminology — making content accessible to everyone.

📶
Low Literacy & Connectivity

SMS support works on basic phones without internet. Menu-based navigation reduces the amount of reading and typing required.

🏆 Our Inclusive Design Commitment

IBANGA HealthBot Rwanda will be tested with young people with disabilities and relevant inclusion stakeholders — including disability-focused youth groups and community health workers — to ensure the platform delivers usability, dignity, and genuine accessibility for all users.

Roadmap

Implementation Plan

MONTHS 1–3

Phase 1 — Development

  • Build chatbot infrastructure
  • Create and validate SRH content with health experts
  • Integrate WhatsApp and SMS APIs
  • Design website and admin dashboard
  • Build Kinyarwanda intent dataset
MONTHS 4–6

Phase 2 — Pilot Testing

  • Launch in selected districts and schools
  • Collect user feedback
  • Monitor frequently asked questions
  • Improve chatbot logic and content
  • Test disability-inclusive features
MONTHS 7–12

Phase 3 — Scale-Up

  • Expand to all 30 districts nationwide
  • Partner with telecom companies
  • Launch school and community campaigns
  • Strengthen clinic referral pathways
  • Add AI-enhanced response quality
What Makes Us Unique

Key Innovations

Kinyarwanda-First

Designed to understand youth expressions, slang, and mixed-language (Kinyarwanda + English/French) naturally.

Hybrid AI System

Medically validated rule-based safety + AI-powered natural responses. Scales to a fully trained Kinyarwanda model.

Disability-Inclusive

The first Rwandan SRH platform with comprehensive accessibility features for users with disabilities.

Emergency Escalation

Detects urgent situations (assault, severe bleeding, pregnancy emergencies) and immediately provides human support pathways.

Offline via SMS

Works on basic phones without internet through SMS — reaching rural youth who may not have smartphones.

Human + AI Hybrid

Simple questions are automated. Complex or sensitive cases connect users to trained health workers or counselors.
