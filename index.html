Inclusive Design

Built for Everyone

IBANGA is specifically designed so that young people with disabilities are never excluded from health information.

👂
Hearing Impairments

Fully text-based — no voice calls needed. Works on WhatsApp chat and SMS.

👁️
Visual Impairments

Screen-reader compatible structure, high-contrast mode, and large text option.

💬
Speech Impairments

Text-based interaction — predefined menus minimize typing effort.

🧠

Simple Language Mode uses plain, short sentences instead of complex medical terms.

📶

SMS support for users without smartphones or internet. Works on basic phones.
