Pregnancy Information

Understanding pregnancy signs, stages, how to stay healthy, and when to seek urgent help — all in one place.

🔍 Early Signs & Symptoms

These are common early signs of pregnancy. Having one or more of these signs does NOT confirm pregnancy — you need a test.

📅 Missed Period

The most common early sign. Especially significant if your periods are usually regular.

🤢 Nausea / Morning Sickness

Can happen at any time of day. Usually begins in the first trimester (weeks 4–8).

🫁 Breast Changes

Breasts may feel tender, swollen, or heavier. Nipples may darken.

😴 Fatigue

Feeling unusually tired is very common due to hormonal changes.

🚻 Frequent Urination

Needing to urinate more often than usual, especially at night.

🍋 Food Cravings / Aversions

Sudden strong desire or dislike for certain foods or smells.

🩸 Other Possible Signs
  • Light spotting (implantation bleeding — lighter than a normal period)
  • Mild abdominal cramps (implantation)
  • Dizziness or mild headaches
  • Mood swings due to hormonal changes

✅ How to Confirm Pregnancy

🧪
Urine Test

Home pregnancy test or at a clinic. Available at pharmacies. Detects hCG hormone from 3–5 days after missed period.

🩸
Blood Test

Done at a health facility. More accurate. Can detect pregnancy earlier than a urine test.

📡
Ultrasound

Scan at a health center. Confirms pregnancy and checks baby's development and heartbeat.

📅 Stages of Pregnancy (3 Trimesters)

Pregnancy usually lasts about 40 weeks (9 months), divided into three stages.

🌱
Weeks 1–12
1st Trimester
  • Major organ development
  • Heart begins to beat
  • Morning sickness common
  • Most critical period
🌿
Weeks 13–26
2nd Trimester
  • Baby grows rapidly
  • Movement can be felt
  • Nausea often improves
  • Baby bump visible
🌸
Weeks 27–40
3rd Trimester
  • Baby fully develops
  • Baby positions for birth
  • Increased discomfort
  • Prepare for labour

🏥 Antenatal Care (ANC)

Regular check-ups during pregnancy are essential for mother and baby's health. In Rwanda, ANC is free at public health centers.

📋 What ANC Includes
  • Blood pressure & weight monitoring
  • Blood tests (anaemia, HIV, syphilis)
  • Ultrasound scans
  • Iron and folic acid supplements
  • Malaria prevention in endemic areas
  • Birth plan and labour preparation
  • Nutrition and lifestyle counseling
🥗 Important Nutrition During Pregnancy
  • Eat iron-rich foods: spinach, beans, meat, eggs
  • Take prescribed folic acid and iron tablets
  • Eat protein: fish, chicken, legumes
  • Drink enough clean water (8+ glasses/day)
  • Avoid: alcohol, tobacco, unprescribed medications

⚠️ Danger Signs — Seek Help Immediately

If you experience any of these during pregnancy, go to a health facility immediately. Do not wait.

🚨 Emergency Warning Signs
  • Severe abdominal or pelvic pain
  • Heavy vaginal bleeding
  • Severe headache that won't go away
  • Blurred or disturbed vision
  • Severe swelling of face, hands, or feet
  • High fever or chills
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Baby not moving or reduced movements (after 28 weeks)
